What is Biochar?

Biochar is a carbon-rich material produced through pyrolysis — the thermal conversion of organic matter at 350–700°C in oxygen-limited conditions. Unlike composting or burning, pyrolysis locks carbon into a crystalline structure that resists biological and chemical degradation for hundreds to thousands of years.

Produced from agricultural waste — crop residues, husks, stalks — that would otherwise release CO₂ through burning or decomposition, biochar permanently sequesters this carbon while simultaneously improving soil fertility, water retention, and microbial activity.

🔬

Engineered Carbon Sequestration

Biochar's aromatic carbon matrix resists decomposition, providing permanence measured in centuries under Verra VM0044 standards.

🌾

Dual Climate & Agricultural Benefit

Every tonne of biochar applied to soil sequesters carbon AND improves crop yields, water retention, and soil microbial health.

♻️

Waste to Value Circular Economy

Agricultural residues that once burned — releasing CO₂ and black carbon — are transformed into a premium carbon removal commodity.

Process

How Biochar CDR Works

A transparent, science-driven process that converts agricultural waste into certified carbon credits — from farm to registry.

STEP 01
🌾

Biomass Collection

Agricultural residues — rice husks, wheat straw, bamboo — are collected from farms, preventing open-field burning.

STEP 02
🔥

Pyrolysis Conversion

Biomass enters a kiln or retort at 350–700°C with limited oxygen. Volatile gases are released; stable carbon remains.

STEP 03
🌱

Soil Application

Biochar is applied to agricultural land, locking carbon underground while improving soil fertility and water retention.

STEP 04
✅

Verification & Credits

Third-party auditors verify removals under Verra VM0044. Carbon credits are issued for each tonne of CO₂e permanently removed.

Standards & Verification

Rigorous Certification Frameworks

Every NatWorks project is anchored in internationally recognised carbon standards, ensuring integrity, transparency, and market credibility.

🏛️

Verra VCS Program

The world's most widely used voluntary carbon standard, ensuring rigorous quantification and verification.

📋

VM0044 Biochar

The dedicated Verra methodology for biochar carbon dioxide removal, covering production monitoring and permanence.

⭐

Gold Standard GS4GG

Premium carbon standard requiring co-benefits for sustainable development goals beyond carbon.

🌍

AFOLU Frameworks

Agriculture, Forestry and Other Land Use methodologies governing soil carbon and afforestation projects.

01

Baseline Establishment

Each project establishes a scientifically rigorous baseline representing carbon stocks and emissions in the absence of the project activity.

02

Additionality Testing

Projects demonstrate that carbon removals would not occur without carbon finance, satisfying the additionality requirement central to carbon market integrity.

03

MRV Framework

Continuous monitoring, periodic reporting, and independent third-party verification ensure that stated removals are real, additional, and permanent.

04

Registry & Issuance

Verified carbon credits are registered on public Verra or Gold Standard registries and issued as Verified Carbon Units (VCUs) or Gold Standard Verified Emission Reductions.

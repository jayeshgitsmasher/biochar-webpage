ESIP_NAT-MON_002 ★ FLAGSHIP PROJECT

Bio-SALAM 1 Biochar Carbon Removal

Methodology: VM0044 Type: Biochar CDR Country: 🇲🇳 Mongolia Annual Volume: 90,192 tCO₂e/yr

NatWorks' flagship biochar carbon dioxide removal project — the largest volume biochar CDR initiative in Central Asia. Agricultural residues from Mongolian farms undergo controlled pyrolysis to produce high-stability biochar, which is then applied to degraded agricultural soils. The project simultaneously sequesters carbon for 1000+ years and restores soil productivity.

EGY-MANG-001

Egyptian Coastal Mangrove Restoration

Methodology: VM0033 Type: Blue Carbon Country: 🇪🇬 Egypt Annual Volume: 15,856 tCO₂e/yr Status: RFR (Ready for Review)

Ambitious coastal restoration initiative along Egypt's coastlines, re-establishing native mangrove ecosystems that sequester carbon in both above-ground biomass and sediment. Mangrove carbon stocks are among the most dense in nature. The project also provides critical coastal protection, fish nursery habitat, and livelihoods for local fishing communities.

EGY-QUTOUF-001

QUTOUF Agroforestry Carbon Project

Methodology: VM0042 Type: Agroforestry Country: 🇪🇬 Egypt Annual Volume: 5,606 VCUs/yr

Integrated agroforestry system combining fruit trees, nitrogen-fixing species, and row crops on previously degraded Egyptian agricultural land. Trees sequester carbon in woody biomass while simultaneously improving farm productivity through shade, wind protection, and nitrogen fixation. The QUTOUF model creates diversified income streams for smallholder farmers.

CDI-ECOB-001

Ecobraiz Clean Cookstoves Initiative

Methodology: VM0050 Type: Clean Cookstoves Country: 🇨🇮 Côte d'Ivoire Annual Volume: 39,577 tCO₂e/yr

Mass distribution of improved biomass cookstoves to rural and peri-urban households in Côte d'Ivoire. The high-efficiency stoves dramatically reduce firewood consumption, cutting both household biomass burning emissions and forest degradation from fuel-wood collection. Women and children benefit most from reduced indoor air pollution and time saved on fuel collection.

Clean Cookstoves VM0050 Côte d'Ivoire Indoor Air Quality Women Empowerment
39,577 tCO₂e/yr Annual Estimated Volume
CDI-AFRI-001

Africharger Electric Mobility Network

Methodology: VM0038 Type: E-Mobility Country: 🇨🇮 Côte d'Ivoire Annual Volume: 10,404 tCO₂e/yr

Infrastructure-led approach to transport decarbonization in Côte d'Ivoire, deploying electric vehicle charging stations to enable the commercial adoption of electric motorcycles, tricycles, and light vehicles. By making EV charging accessible and affordable, the project accelerates the shift away from fossil-fuel two and three-wheelers — the dominant transport mode for millions of Ivorians.

E-Mobility VM0038 Côte d'Ivoire Clean Transport Urban Air Quality
10,404 tCO₂e/yr Annual Estimated Volume

ELECTRAN Electric Cooking Project
